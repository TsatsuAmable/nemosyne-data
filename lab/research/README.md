# Research obligation authority

Nemosyne-data owns the derived research-obligation registry and its RFL review cycle. Nemosyne owns the governing product vision, design, architecture, roadmap, product runtime, generic product simulator adapters, and native QV adjudication.

The lab reads a pinned Nemosyne specimen and governing documents, hashes those inputs, derives review packets, schedules research/adversarial work, and preserves evidence. Findings may propose Nemosyne roadmap/design changes, but cannot mutate those authorities directly.

`NEMOSYNE_SOURCE_ROOT` selects the pinned Nemosyne checkout used to build a review packet. Routine automation must resolve an exact Nemosyne commit before review.
