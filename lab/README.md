# Nemosyne Experimental Laboratory

This tree owns portable research orchestration, benchmark corpora, perturbation campaigns, experimental simulation scenarios, evidence collection, and reproducible analysis bundles.

The nemosyne repository remains the specimen and product authority. Product runtime, Rust/WASM analytical authority, production lifecycle/interaction systems, and generic development simulation remain there.

Experiments must pin the nemosyne build/commit they exercise. Research artifacts here do not become product authority merely by existing.
